Frequently Asked Questions

Home › FAQ

How long does shipping take?

+

Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days within the US and 7–14 days internationally. Express shipping (1–2 days) is available at checkout. All orders over $75 qualify for free standard shipping.

What is your return policy?

+

We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy on all items in original, unused condition with tags attached. Beauty products must be sealed and unopened for returns. Simply contact our team and we'll send you a prepaid return label.

Are your products authentic and cruelty-free?

+

Yes — 100%. Every product sold through Lumière & Co. is authenticated, and we only partner with brands that are cruelty-free certified. Our jewelry and accessories come with certificates of authenticity.

Can I track my order?

+

Absolutely. Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number and link. You can also track your order anytime from your account dashboard under "My Orders."

Do you offer gift wrapping?

+

Yes! Gift wrapping is available at checkout for a small fee. Your order will arrive in our signature blush and gold gift box with a handwritten note card. Perfect for birthdays, anniversaries, and special occasions.

Which payment methods do you accept?

+

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Klarna (buy now, pay later in 4 installments). All transactions are protected by 256-bit SSL encryption.

Do you ship internationally?

+

Yes! We ship to over 80 countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination. Import duties and customs fees are the responsibility of the customer and vary by country.

Still have questions?

Our team is happy to help. Reach out and we'll get back to you within a few hours.
